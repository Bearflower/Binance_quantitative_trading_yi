4. **M4 formal alert:** Remains closed until M3 passes, all (b) ACs pass, and (c) ACs plus delivery/content review pass. No current evidence supports flipping `mode` to `alert`.

The acceptance design is directionally sound because it separates mechanism, online reliability, statistical effect and release. **Two definitions need to be frozen before validation:** (a) the acceptable numeric-reference availability, with both calendar-time and opportunity-conditioned denominators; (b) numerical limits for alert burden, 60-second actionable coverage, normal/urgent false-positive rates, per-regime event counts and confidence-interval width. The report's `eligible_event_count` counts emitted non-health alerts with known decision times. It cannot by itself estimate opportunity coverage; independent replay must enumerate all first market crossings, including missed ones.

## Feasibility and next decisions

- **Time is necessary but insufficient.** The oldest 27 complete sample days become 30 only after three more complete days, assuming no missing row. The future validation span cannot be inferred from that date because raw-ID, queue and reference evidence start later and the freeze is pending.
- **Individual evidence clocks differ.** If there are no further gaps, the existing sample axis first reaches 30 complete Beijing days at 2026-11-07 00:00. The first aggregate-ID attestation covers trades only back to 2026-11-01 15:05, so its first complete Beijing day is Nov 2 and a 30-day continuous chain cannot mature before Dec 2. New reference-status evidence began Nov 4 15:05; its first complete day is Nov 5 and 30 full days cannot mature before Dec 5. These are lower bounds for individual evidence streams, not a formal launch date or permission to overlap development and independent validation segments.
- **Signal scarcity is currently the larger uncertainty.** One development crossing and one historic emitted URGENT cannot bound false-positive or actionable-coverage rates. With one independent Bernoulli event, even a perfect 1/1 outcome has Wilson 95% lower bound about 20.7%; zero false positives in one event has upper bound about 79.3%. For illustration, zero false positives in 35 independent events only brings the Wilson upper bound below 10%; clustered market episodes provide less independent information.
- **Not proven impossible.** The M1 event showed a useful 243-second candidate lead, but it was one episode. The 60.8% numeric-reference time ceiling is a distinct product constraint. It cannot be repaired by silently extending `reference.max_age_seconds` or treating unsent calculated boundaries as sent. Either the hourly strategy supplies confirmed numeric references more consistently, or the product explicitly accepts limited availability. Both choices require a deliberate requirement/acceptance decision.
- **Product target selected later on 2026-11-04:** ≥90% VALID numeric-reference time across the full independent validation period. The observed 60.8% upper bound does not meet this target. It does not prove the target impossible in future data, but the current hourly strategy produces no numeric grid parameters in several strong-trend and volatility states; a safe redesign of reference supply is required before assuming the target is attainable. A notification without numeric boundaries, an unsent calculation, or an expired prior snapshot cannot count as VALID. This target is recorded; the remaining M3 thresholds and the independent validation plan are not frozen.
- **No fixed launch date can be promised.** A 30-day calendar window may finish without enough slow-approach, fast-jump or sideways events. Keep shadow and collect data until both the preregistered effect and reliability gates pass; if they fail, retain only the separately labeled limited factual/health function.
